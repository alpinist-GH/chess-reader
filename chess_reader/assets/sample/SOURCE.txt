Bundled sample books — one of each supported format, both public domain.

- "Chess Strategy - Edward Lasker.pdf"
  Edward Lasker, "Chess Strategy", 2nd edition (1915). Public domain (published
  >100 years ago, no copyright notice). A games book in English DESCRIPTIVE
  notation (P-K4, Kt-QB3, ...) with diagrams — it exercises both diagram
  detection and the descriptive-notation reader (see descriptive_notation.dart).
  This free PDF edition adds diagrams (José Garcia) over the original text and
  was reformatted by Fergus Duniho.

- "Chess History - Bird.epub"
  H. E. Bird, "Chess History and Reminiscences" (1893). Public domain. Prose
  history; Project Gutenberg branding stripped.

If you replace either file, keep the asset paths referenced in pubspec.yaml
(assets/sample/) and the kSampleBooks list in book_import.dart, and update this
note.
