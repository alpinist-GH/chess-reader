Privacy Policy

ChessBook Reader
Last updated: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ChessBook Reader ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use the iOS application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

ChessBook Reader does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not create user accounts or require registration.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or location.
  • We do not track your reading activity or the books you import.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs.

2. Files You Import

PDF and EPUB files you open in the App are processed entirely on your device. No file content is ever transmitted to our servers or any third party. Converted diagrams and extracted text are stored only in the App's local cache on your device and are deleted when you remove the App.

3. Camera Access

The App may request access to your device camera to let you scan a physical chess book and import it as a PDF. Camera images are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded or stored beyond your session. You can revoke camera permission at any time in iOS Settings → ChessBook Reader.

4. In-App Purchases (RevenueCat)

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Unlock") processed through Apple's App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage purchase receipts and entitlements. RevenueCat may collect limited non-personal technical data (app version, device type, purchase event timestamps) to validate your purchase and restore it on reinstall. No personally identifiable data is shared with RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple's App Store privacy practices apply to payment processing: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Chess Engine (Stockfish)

The App includes the open-source Stockfish chess engine, which runs entirely on your device. No moves, positions, or analysis results are transmitted externally.

6. Third-Party SDKs Summary

SDK Purpose Data sent off-device
RevenueCat Purchase validation Anonymous purchase receipt
Apple StoreKit Payment processing Handled entirely by Apple
Apple Vision / ML Kit On-device OCR None – runs on-device only

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. It contains no social features, accounts, or data submission forms.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
