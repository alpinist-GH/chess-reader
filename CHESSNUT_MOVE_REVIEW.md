Validation: 20 new regression cases plus the original 38 Chessnut tests pass. The full Flutter suite reports **185 passed, 1 failed**: the existing Stockfish process test cannot locate its required engine binary (`test/engine_test.dart:90`). No engine download was performed. Static analysis of the Chessnut feature, shared game session, and changed regression/prerequisite tests is clean. The shared board widget test now injects the fake BLE transport instead of contacting the native plugin.

Hardware acceptance remains outstanding. No real-board or signed platform build validation was performed. The fake uses synthetic packets and immediate or deliberately delayed matching FEN reports; those are software test inputs, not hardware captures. The production gate must remain disabled until firmware-specific completion/interruption behavior, stop encoding, complete transfers, and current piece availability are established and implemented. Merely changing the capability flag is insufficient.

The [published Chessnut protocol](https://github.com/chessnutech/chess_move_api) says FEN reports are disabled during auto-moves and contains inconsistent stop-command length instructions. It supplies piece coordinates and battery values but no explicit presence flag. These unresolved contracts are the reason for the hardware gate required by the saved plan.
